About us

A local practice, from Irinjalakuda to the High Court

Adv. Abijith Prasad is an advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Kerala and practises under the name AP Associates from Irinjalakuda, Thrissur. The office handles matters before the civil and criminal courts of Thrissur district, the Family Courts, the Motor Accidents Claims Tribunals and the High Court of Kerala.

The office functions from KJ Complex, opposite the Civil Station, Irinjalakuda, and attends the courts at Irinjalakuda, Kodungallur, Chalakudy, Thrissur and Kunnamkulam, as well as the High Court of Kerala at Ernakulam.
